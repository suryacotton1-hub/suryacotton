The Founder

Mr. R. Meenakshi Sundaram

Founder & Chairman

Born and raised in Tamil Nadu, Mr. R. Meenakshi Sundaram started his entrepreneurial journey in 2013 with a single store in K. Pudur, Madurai — driven by one belief: quality menswear should be affordable for every man.

With hands-on dedication and deep customer empathy, he built SuryaCotton step by step — from students to politicians, serving men from all walks of life. Today, the brand operates three successful outlets including a wholesale hub in Sellur.

His leadership style blends business insight with social responsibility, earning not just profits, but deep trust from thousands of loyal customers across multiple districts.

"If you want to become rich, your first step shouldn't be 'how to earn money'… It should be 'how to earn a name and reputation'. Once you earn respect, money will come easily."
— Mr. R. Meenakshi Sundaram
What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To redefine men's fashion by offering premium-quality clothing at prices every man can afford — from students to professionals, from laborers to leaders. Every garment we offer is handpicked for fabric quality, comfort, and the specific needs of South Indian men.

Our Vision

To grow SuryaCotton into one of India's most trusted menswear brands — expanding nationwide through digital innovation and franchising, while staying rooted in our Madurai heritage. We aim to create employment, uplift local communities, and preserve traditional textile craftsmanship.

Our Brand

Crafted For Every Gentleman

SuryaCottans was founded with one mission — to bring premium-quality menswear to every modern Indian man. From the busy streets of Tamil Nadu to boardrooms across India, our garments are worn by men who refuse to settle for less.

Every collection is a blend of heritage craftsmanship and contemporary design, built to make you feel powerful the moment you put it on.

We treat every customer like family. Every transaction is a promise. Our shops aren't just retail outlets — they're spaces of belonging, where people are respected, heard, and served with honesty.
