📋 EmailJS Template Setup Guide

  1. Click "Create Service" in your EmailJS modal (as shown in your screenshot).
  2. In EmailJS left sidebar, click "Email Templates" → "Create New Template".
  3. In Template Settings:
    • Subject: Order Confirmed: #{{order_id}} - SuryaCotton
    • To Email: {{to_email}} (CRITICAL: Do NOT leave your own email here; set it to {{to_email}} so customer receives it!)
    • From Name: SuryaCotton
    • Reply To: suryacotton1@gmail.com
  4. In the template editor, switch to HTML mode (or click </>) and paste the luxury email template below, then click "Save".
  5. Copy the Template ID (e.g. template_xxxxxxx) and Public Key (Account → API Keys), and paste them above!
